What your hotel gets, and how we both know it worked

Your hotel gets

  • Members who stay ten minutes after their workout
  • A drink chosen for each member, on the road you pick
  • A free recovery guide for every member who checks in
  • The numbers after: who came, what they chose, who wants more

How we know it worked

  • Before the day, we agree two numbers together. For example, how many members check in and how many ask for a next time.
  • Hit them, and we book a second date.
  • Miss them, and we stop. Nothing to renew.
