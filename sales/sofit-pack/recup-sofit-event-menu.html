Who should skip or swap (asked in the check-in)

Pregnant or breastfeeding · heart condition (e.g. after a heart attack) · on any medication
  • No amino acid drink mix. We serve a version without it, or water.
On blood thinners (e.g. warfarin)
  • No acai berry smoothie (the shake contains vitamin K). See your doctor first.
  • Fish, milk, soy, peanut or oat allergy: we steer you to a drink without it.

Kitchen notes

Cross-contactPeanut drinks are blended in a separate jug. All drinks are made at one station where milk, soy, oat, peanut and fish ingredients are present. Guests with severe allergies: please speak to our team first.
Food safetyMade fresh on site, served in sealed cups with lids, kept chilled. Ingredient labels available on request.
ClaimsThese are drinks, not medicine. We make no health claims.
