Plain Language Mandate

One place for how M.O.E. and RECUP.STN talk to customers. Every person and every AI agent reads from here before they write. If it is not in here, it is not the rule.

1.0 · Commander's intent

We think in systems. We speak like people.

Our internal words (Wolf) are for us. Customers only ever hear the plain version (Shepherd). Jargon is a shield. Plain words are a bridge. A customer has 7 seconds to feel understood, so we lead with their problem in their words, then give a fix of 3 lines or less.

2.0 · Objectives
O1Same words everywherePosts, DMs, booth, site, calls, and every AI agent use one live lexicon.
O2Nothing ships uncheckedEvery customer-facing line passes the gate check below before it goes out.
O3It gets better every weekMisses become proposals. The founder approves. Everyone gets the update at once.

Scope. Anything a customer sees, hears or reads. Staff-only screens (God Terminal, jug recipes, the Armory) can keep internal words.

    3.0 · Procedure 2 · The rules

    Eight rules

    R1
    Say what it does, not what we call it.
    Fuel System ArchitectureLose belly fat, keep your muscle
    R2
    Short words. Sentences under 15 words. The checker flags anything over 20.
    Postprandial somnolenceFeeling slow after lunch
    R3
    One name per thing, at most. A product can have a name (THE BUILD, RECUP 90). Everything around it is plain.
    Deploy your Fuel ProtocolGet your plan and products
    R4
    A number only if we can show where it came from. One study supports a claim. It never proves it.
    Decision speed drops 20% at 3:10pmMost people dip between 2 and 4pm
    R5
    We help. We never treat, heal or cure. Disease words appear only in the safety question, never in a claim.
    Heals your gutSettles your stomach
    R6
    Say who it is not for, plainly, before they pay.
    Not for you right now if you're pregnant or breastfeeding
    R7
    Answer the five questions. What you get. How long. What you do. How much. Is it safe for me.
    90 days. One measured meal a day. RM 1,600, free with a starter pack.
    R8
    Plain punctuation. Periods and commas. No dashes, semicolons or exclamation marks.
    Ready? Let's go!Ready when you are.
    3.0 · Procedure 3 · The flow

    One path for every program

    B2C (RECUP 90, runners) and B2B (gyms, teams) both follow these five steps. Each step has a gate. Don't move someone forward until it passes.

    01A post catches themTheir problem in their words. Content is a gift, not a pitch.
    GatePassed the gate check above.
    02They comment or DMWe reply with one warm line and one question. No price, no program name.
    GateThey answered our question.
    03Whale questionsA few quick questions. Runners already did The Diagnostic, so they only fill in the HQ booking form.
    GateWhale rule passes and safety is clear.
    0415-minute callWe go through their answers or body scan. Then we explain the program and price.
    GateThey say yes, or we let them go kindly.
    05DeployThey get their plan and products. Week 1 check-in is booked before they leave.
    GateFirst check-in is in the calendar.
    B2C · RECUP 90 (public card)
    • Weeks 1–2: settle your stomach, sleep better, steady energy.
    • Weeks 3–13: lose belly fat and keep your muscle by hitting your daily protein number. One measured meal a day makes that easy.
    • Cost: RM 1,600 coaching. Free when you get your starter pack from us.
    • Where: RECUP.STN HQ in Bukit Damansara, or a video call.
    Not for you right now if
    • You're pregnant or breastfeeding, unless your doctor says OK.
    • You have stage 3, 4 or 5 kidney disease.
    • You're having chemo or radiotherapy.
    • You take blood thinners like warfarin. Ask your doctor before fish oil or garlic supplements.
    3.0 · Procedure 4 · Whale Diagnostic

    Whale questions

    Draft · awaiting founder

    Drafted from The Diagnostic, the Three Shepherd Questions and the Metabolic Profile Schema (T-015). It replaces the FORMOH Audit for non-runners. Send as a short form or ask them in DMs, one at a time.

    1. What's the one thing about your body or energy that bugs you most right now?Example answers: crash after lunch, belly fat that won't move, sore after every run.
      Friction
    2. When does it show up the most?Mornings, after lunch, after runs, at night.
      Pattern
    3. What have you already tried?Diets, gym, apps, coffee, nothing yet.
      Effort
    4. If this was fixed 90 days from now, what would be different for you?
      Aspiration
    5. Do any of these apply: pregnant or breastfeeding, kidney disease stage 3 to 5, chemo or radiotherapy, blood thinners?
      Safety
    6. If the plan makes sense on our call, could you start within 2 weeks?
      Ready
    7. B2B only: How many people on your team or in your gym, and what happens when they crash or quit?Replaces Q4 for gyms and teams.
      Cost of pain
    Whale · book the 15-minute call
    • Clear problem (Q1) and they've tried something (Q3).
    • Ready within 2 weeks (Q6 yes).
    • Safety clear (Q5 none).
    Not yet · keep giving, no call
    • Vague problem or not ready: send one useful free piece and check back in 30 days.
    • Safety yes: thank them, refer to their doctor first. Never sell.

    4.0 · Decision record
    
      
    What we've decided
    
      
    
        
    5 Oct 2026RECUP.STN HQ replaces "HealthBar" in everything customers see. The internal key stays healthbar.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026RECUP 90 is the public name. "The Sovereign Health Protocol" stays internal.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026"Measured meal" replaces "shake". The partner brand is not named in public until we've tested how people react.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026The meal replacement study (Flechtner-Mors, 2010) is internal only. Citing it would mean naming the funder. Recheck the exact paper before any use.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026The "20% decision speed drop" and "3:10 PM" figures are retired. They have no single source. We use "most people dip between 2 and 4pm".
    
        
    5 Oct 2026Disease words (fertility, PCOS, thyroid, blood sugar) were removed from the RECUP.STN HQ booking form. A required safety question was added in their place.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026One flow for every program: post, reply, Whale questions, 15-minute call, deploy. Runners skip the questions because The Diagnostic already covers them.
    
        
    5 Oct 2026The live RECUP.STN site was rewritten to this mandate: The Diagnostic, the member card, the Repair Manual, the posters, the marketing kit and the playbook.
