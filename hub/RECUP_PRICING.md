## The two roads

| | Road A · Members' privilege | Road B · Members pay |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays | The venue | Each member, RM18 per cup |
| Venue fee | Yes, per morning (below) | None |
| Best for | Hotels and premium clubs that want to give members something | Studios and gyms that want zero cost |
| Watch out | Agree the minimum before the day | Hotels may ask for a share; offer none for the pilot |

## Road A · the price per morning

**Formula:** station fee **RM300** + **RM16 per cup poured**, with a **minimum of 25 cups**.

- The station fee covers two of our team for about 2.5 hours, set-up and pack-down, the check-in, and the numbers report after.
- RM16 per cup is RM2 below the member price of RM18. The venue gets a volume price for paying for everyone.
- The minimum protects us if few members turn up. The venue pays for 25 cups even if fewer are poured.
- Billed after the morning, on the cups actually poured (or the minimum, whichever is higher).

| Members who take a drink | Price to the venue |
|---|---|
| 25 (the minimum) | RM700 |
| 30 | RM780 |
| 40 | RM940 |
| 50 | RM1,100 |
| 60 | RM1,260 |

**Ballpark to say on a call:** "Around RM700 to RM1,100 for a morning, depending on how many members join. You only pay for the drinks we actually pour, with a minimum of 25."
