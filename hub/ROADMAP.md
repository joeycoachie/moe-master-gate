## 1. The end in mind

**What we are building.** An Enterprise Operating System for Human Performance ("How Far We Have Come"), sold as the Triad (D-014):

| Pillar | What it is | Ours today |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware (KIN) | Clinical movement arcs and studio method | Armory arcs (KIN-P016, KIN-P019), Reformer and hardware doctrines |
| Fuel (MET) | Precision nutrition and recovery | RECUP.STN (Ops Healthbar), Product Academy, Herbalife supply (SYS-D023) |
| Software (SYS) | The system that runs it and remembers what works | M.O.E. Terminal, Intelligence Archive, hub |

**The end state.** Duplication. A trained operator runs a studio and fuel hub on the M.O.E. system without the founder in the room ("Box in a Box"; P-031 Phantom Equity). Each operator pays a software licence and a royalty. Every session they log makes the protocols better for all of them.

**The honest maths of "billionaire".** A company is usually valued at a multiple of what flows through it. A billion-dollar outcome needs roughly **US$100M a year** moving through the system. One way that could add up (an example, not a plan): **5,000 operator nodes × about US$1,700 a month** across licence, royalty and fuel margin. No single product gets there. Only the network does, and that is a 10 to 15 year climb. This is the money side of the 40-year Summit (D-004).

## 7. Positioning: the gap we fill, and the safety floor

**The gap (hypothesis, from our own files).** Studios already buy software for the *front desk*: booking, payments, memberships. Nothing manages *what happens on the floor*: whether the programming is right for each body, whether 10 instructors teach to one standard, and whether a client with a knee or RA is progressing or quietly getting hurt. Today that lives in the owner's head, Google Sheets and WhatsApp (7-Year-Old Plan, USP 1 and 2). We fill it with three things:

1. **Clinical arcs any instructor can run**, with regressions built in (Armory; KIN-P016, KIN-P019 RA and knee overrides).
2. **Instructor quality control** (V.A.E. audits, SYS-T050; the Terminal).
3. **A per-client record that proves results**, which becomes the case study that sells the next studio.

The **moat** is the Triad on top (SYS-D023): the same client record also routes fuel and recovery (RECUP.STN), which booking software will never do.

**What is not proven yet.** One studio used it and left on 9 Oct. Until we know *why* Optimum Fit left (question 5), the gap is a hypothesis. That answer is the most valuable data we own right now.

**Evidence (founder, 10 Oct).** Optimum Fit did not leave because the product failed. They wanted the 90-day Phase 1 build to run forever without paying the monthly management retainer (Tier 2). They treated cloud software as a one-off asset. Our own files already had the rule. **P-042** says "Access > Hours" and **T-044** says "Capital-Forward pricing". The Optimum Fit deal let the build and the running cost be bought separately, so the rule was never enforced.

**Dream buyer:** a high-agency studio owner (the Protagonist) who values systems, data and accountable execution, not cheap labour or an app that runs itself.
**Who we refuse:** anyone wanting enterprise systems at freelance rates; owners who hide behind "busy" to avoid decisions; anyone expecting free hosting and management outside an active retainer.

**The BC3 offer (answer to question 4).** The "90-Day Setup" is Step 3 of the 7-Year-Old Plan: installing the **Kinetic Studio OS** (Terminal + Armory + client tracking + instructor checks) into **one studio**. It is the offer for the Kinetic node only. Fuel and Software come later (D-002, one Base Camp at a time). The Optimum Fit lesson fixes its shape. **The setup is never sold without the licence:**

| Part | What it is | How it's paid |
|---|---|---|
| 90-Day Setup | Install, load the studio's classes, train instructors on the arcs | One fee, 50% before day 1 |
| Studio Licence | Hosting, updates, new arcs, governance. **The system stops if this stops** | Monthly from day 1, minimum 12 months |

The numbers are still open: what Optimum Fit paid for Phase 1 and what Tier 2 cost are the best starting point.

**The safety floor: a 9-to-5 is allowed (SYS-D025 Barbell).** Our risk doctrine says 85% safe anchor, 15% bold bets. The rule is simple: **if this month's income is under 3k, take a job.** It's the 85%, not a failure. Pick it with four tests:

1. **Pays the 3k floor every month**, so the business stops being a survival bet.
2. **Puts you inside the buyer's world**: it works as paid market research for the 15%.
3. **Trains the constraint.** Ours is selling and packaging software, not building it.
4. **Uses proof you already have**, because the network is thin. The Terminal and the Optimum Fit build are a portfolio.

| Role | 1 Floor | 2 Inside the buyer | 3 Trains the constraint | 4 Uses your proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation / customer success at a studio or health software company | Yes | Yes: you see why studios buy and cancel | Yes: packaging, onboarding, renewals | Yes: you did this for Optimum Fit |
| Operations or systems lead at a multi-site studio, clinic or gym | Yes | Yes: you are the buyer | Partly | Yes |
| Junior product / solutions role at a wellness platform | Yes | Partly | Yes: pricing and product | Partly |
| Programme or operations role at a wellness resort, retreat or wellness-residential developer | Yes | Yes: a much bigger buyer | Partly | Weak today: thin network |

**Avoid:** pure coding (AI makes code cheap; your edge is the domain plus the system) and only teaching hours (the freelancer era).

**Wellness real estate and retreats.** It's a large, growing market, and it has the same floor gap. Retreats and wellness residences sell "programmes", run them with freelance instructors, and keep no record once the guest leaves. Our arcs + client record + RECUP.STN fuel answer exactly that. It's a **channel for BC4–BC5, not a pivot now**: the sales cycles are long, the buyers are developers who buy from operators they know, and we have no network there yet. The cheap test meanwhile is one Malaysian retreat or wellness resort running a short programme on our arcs, or the fourth job in the table above.
