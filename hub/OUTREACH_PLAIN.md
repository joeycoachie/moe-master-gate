# Outreach in plain words

The Phase Action Protocol (Archive gate G-25) says the same five steps four ways, one per
audience. Internal names stay inside. Everything a studio owner, a recruit or a follower reads
passes SYS-D028 (`node archive/scripts/gate.mjs "text"`). Written 17 Oct 2026.

| Internal name | Plain name | What actually happens |
|---|---|---|
| Targeting | Find | Pick 10 studios that care about results but run on WhatsApp and spreadsheets |
| Infiltration (3-Line Rule, Axiomatic Hook) | Say hello | A short message with one ask: a 15-minute call |
| The X-Ray (UEF Track 1) | Listen | Ask questions, say back what you heard, no pitch |
| The Blueprint (UEF Track 2) | Plan | Hand them a 90-day plan built from their own words |
| The Handover | Start | If it fits, sign the 90-Day Setup |

## 1. To a studio owner (first message)

> You opened your studio to coach, not to chase spreadsheets. When every instructor tracks
> clients their own way, details get lost and clients drift. We set up one simple system for
> your floor: the same programme, every client's progress on record. Can I show you in 15
> minutes where your studio is losing time?

On the call, the five steps are what they feel, not what we call them:
"I'll ask a few questions and just listen. Then I'll send you a 90-day plan built from what you
told me. If it fits, we start. If it doesn't, you keep the plan."

## 2. To a recruit (how we grow)

> We grow in five steps. Find, say hello, listen, plan, start. Your job starts with the first
> three. Most people rush to the pitch. We don't pitch until we can describe the owner's problem
> better than they can. That is the whole skill, and we will train you in it.

## 3. On social media (first person, story first)

> Most studio owners I meet didn't open a studio to do admin.
> They opened it because they love watching someone move without pain.
> Then the business grows. The spreadsheets grow faster.
> So now we do one thing first. We sit with an owner for 15 minutes and just listen.
> Then we hand back a simple 90-day plan.
> No big pitch. Just a clear next step.
> If you run a studio and this sounds familiar, send me the word PLAN.

Tone note: matching a specific creator's voice (e.g. Eileen Ngu) needs 2 or 3 of her real posts
as a reference. Until then this is a warm, plain, story-first voice.

## 4. For us (the system view)

The five steps are one loop, and each step feeds a number. Track the counts every Friday:

`studios found → messages sent → replies → calls → plans sent → signed`

- The only number fully in our control is **messages sent**. Protect it daily.
- Every call is data even when it doesn't sign: the friction they name becomes the next message.
- One signed 90-Day Setup closes the BC3 exit gate. One is the target, not ten.
- The plan we send is an asset either way: it shows our thinking to an owner who may sign later.
