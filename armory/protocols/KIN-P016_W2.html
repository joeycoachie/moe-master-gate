Commander's Intent // Week 2 Biomechanical Focus

4s Eccentric Scapular Braking → Stable-Base Anterior/Posterior Slings → 5s Long Stretch

Primary Target Pec Minor / Lower Traps / Serratus
Tempo Mandate 1s Concentric : 4s Eccentric

The Week 2 Architectural Rule: Static stretching alone cannot permanently resolve rounded shoulders; we must eccentrically load both the tight anterior chest fascia and the posterior scapular stabilizers. Phase 1 calibrates the 4-second eccentric braking tempo using the STOTT 5 Principles (and Foam Roller/Yoga Block fulcrums on Mat). Phase 2 separates sagittal glute-anchored thoracic extension from stable-base split-stance eccentric pulls/flies (eradicating moving-carriage split conflicts). Phase 3 executes the unified ascending closed-chain series under a strict 4-to-5 second eccentric tempo.

1. Technical Gate Verification

Gate 01: 4s Eccentric Scapular Braking (Phase 1)

Did the client maintain continuous 4-second eccentric deceleration without letting the springs/band jerk their humeral heads forward or popping the lower ribs off the roller/mat?

Gate 02: Glute Anchor & Stable-Base Pull (Phase 2)

Did the client engage glutes and press the pubic bone down *before* lifting into Prone Extension (M1), and keep the split-stance pelvis dead square during the eccentric release (M2)?

Gate 03: Eccentric Closed-Chain Series Ceiling (Phase 3)

At which lever length did the serratus anterior begin to wing or the lumbar spine begin to sag under the 4-to-5 second eccentric tempo?
