Commander's Intent // KIN-P019 W3

Isometric Tendon Analgesia & Soft-Knee Hinge Re-entry

In Week 3, we re-introduce the hip hinge pattern. However, to prevent the autonomic sympathetic response (piloerection/"goosebumps") caused by sciatic-tibial nerve traction, we strictly enforce a 30° soft-knee bend (The Wall-Tap Hinge). Straight legs are banned.

To address patellofemoral sensitivity, we deploy Shallow Isometric Holds (e.g., Spanish Squats / Wall Sits). Heavy isometric holds at a shallow angle (30-45°) create immediate tendon analgesia (pain relief for the patellar tendon) without the shear forces of deep flexion. The Override profile avoids deep squats entirely.
