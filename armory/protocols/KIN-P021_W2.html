Commander's Intent // The Lighter Body Programme

Gravity Offload On-Ramp: W2 Spine That Moves

The Safe Harbour Rule: The client remains deconditioned and physically uncomfortable. Week 2 builds on breath and introduces gentle supine and seated articulation to bring life back to the flat spine. We also introduce side-hip (glute medius) strength to resolve the high-side hike and mid-back awakening for the forward head. NO awkward floor transitions (Offload Override active).

Biomechanical Target (The Flat Spine): The spine moves like a solid block. We use the apparatus to assist flexion and extension. The locking knee is protected by prioritizing open-chain or soft-knee closed-chain work. Note: The Ladder Barrel apex straddle has been removed from this profile. Getting on and off a 36-inch apex safely is physically threatening for a deconditioned bariatric client. We substitute with the Wunda Chair or High Box for all seated articulation.
