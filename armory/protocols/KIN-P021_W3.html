Commander's Intent // The Lighter Body Programme (KIN-P021 W3)

Gravity Offload On-Ramp: W3 Even Both Sides

The Safe Harbour Rule: In Week 3, we move from bilateral support into controlled unilateral work to balance the high side (elevated hip and shoulder), the locking knee, the foot that rolls out, and the restricted rib rotation. Every client side is read from Station 07—never assumed.

Apparatus & Safety Rationale: Raised apparatus (Reformer, Cadillac, Wunda Chair, High Box) and supported TRX work keep transitions dignified. The Ladder Barrel apex straddle is omitted for this archetype because mounting and dismounting a 36-inch apex safely creates fall risk for deconditioned adults; all seated work runs on the Chair, Cadillac edge, or High Box. Every station also carries a floor option for mat-only studios.
