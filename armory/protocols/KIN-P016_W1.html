Gate Prerequisite 5 Principles Before Load

The Architectural Rule: Never load Long Box Pulling Straps or full Planks cold. We first isolate pure scapular protraction, retraction, and depression without moving the spine (Phase 1), then integrate thoracic extension and split-stance contralateral rows from a stable foot base (Phase 2), and finally execute the unified closed-chain progression from Elephant → Down Stretch → Up Stretch → Long Stretch (Phase 3).
