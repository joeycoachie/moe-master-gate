Commander's Intent // KIN-P019 W4

Gait & Coronal Integration

Week 4 is the graduation phase of the Knee/Nerve arc. We must integrate the isolated hip strength from previous weeks into functional walking propulsion (Gait). This demands rebuilding the medial arch (Tripod Foot) to prevent valgus collapse, and utilizing lateral step-downs and forward step-ups.

Triage T1 Doctrine: In Phase 2 and 3, overrides inject safety cues without removing the heavy load. If the client clears Phase 3 Gait Propulsion without anterior knee pain or sciatic tension, they graduate from the KIN-P019 Arc back to standard systemic loading.
