Commander's Intent // The Lighter Body Programme

Gravity Offload On-Ramp: W1 Breath & Finding The Curve

The Safe Harbour Rule: The client is deconditioned and feels "stiff and heavy". Our sole objective is to make the body feel lighter by using the apparatus to offload gravity. We must maintain dignity: NO awkward floor transitions (Offload Override active).

Biomechanical Target (The Flat Spine): The intake reveals a completely flat spine with a forward head, a high-side hike, and a locking knee. We use gentle articulation to *find* the spinal curves. Apparatus Breadth: We now utilize the full studio. The Chair provides dignified seated options. The TRX allows supported standing or prone work. The Ladder Barrel is included only for seated/standing stretches where the client does not need to awkwardly climb or invert.
