Commander's Intent // Portal name (proposed): The Lighter Body Programme

Gravity Offload On-Ramp: W1 Breath & Finding The Curve

The Safe Harbour Rule: The client is deconditioned, overwhelmed, and feels "stiff and heavy". There is zero diet pressure. Our sole objective in Week 1 is to make the body feel lighter using the apparatus to offload gravity. We must maintain dignity: NO awkward floor transitions (Offload Override active).

Biomechanical Target (The Flat Spine): The intake reveals a completely flat spine (cervical, upper thoracic, and lumbar) with a forward head, a one-sided hike (pelvis and shoulder high on the same side), and one knee that locks back into hyperextension. We use gentle articulation to find the spinal curves, avoiding heavy standing loads that would shear the locked knee.

Sides: which hip is high, which knee locks and which foot rolls out come from the client's Station 07 sheet in the client file, never from this card. "High side" below means the client's elevated pelvis/shoulder side.
