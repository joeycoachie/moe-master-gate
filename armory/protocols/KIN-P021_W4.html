Commander's Intent // The Lighter Body Programme (KIN-P021 W4 — Arc Exit)

Gravity Offload On-Ramp: W4 Up to Standing & Hand-Over Gate

The Hand-Over Mandate: Week 4 transfers three weeks of supported strength into gravity without yo-yoing between floor and standing. We sequence strictly Seated → Supported Standing → Upright Control. Clients practice staged High-Box Sit-to-Stand, supported standing pulls, and floor-based Chair pedal presses (never climbing on top of the Chair yet).

Apparatus Rationale: Ladder Barrel apex straddling remains excluded for fall safety. Every station uses dignified seated or supported standing heights, plus a guaranteed floor/prop alternative for mat-only environments. Clearing all three Phase 5 exit gates hands the client over to a standing arc (such as KIN-P016 or KIN-P019).
