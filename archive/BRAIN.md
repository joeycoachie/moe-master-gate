Nothing manages *what happens on the floor*: whether the programming is right for each body, whether 10 instructors teach to one standard, and whether a client with a knee or RA is progressing or quietly getting hurt. 2. **Instructor quality control** (V.A.E. audits, SYS-T050; the Terminal).
3. **A per-client record that proves results**, which becomes the case study that sells the next studio.

The **moat** is the Triad on top (SYS-D023): the same client record also routes fuel and recovery (RECUP.STN), which booking software will never do.

## 3b. How we think with high-value operators (HVO Playbook)


Written 20 Oct 2026. For the founder and every agent that drafts for him. Nothing here is a new
framework (Rule B): each lens is a protocol we already own, named by its Archive ID.
Messages to HVOs follow the house rule: professional, short, plain, one ask (SYS-D028).

### 1. The shift in one line

An average operator asks "how do I close this person?". A systems operator asks
**"what does this conversation add to the machine, whether or not they say yes?"**
Every HVO touch feeds the loop in ROADMAP §4: Crucible → Capture → Codify → Productize → Distribute.
A "no" that names their real problem is data. A "yes" with no record is wasted.

### 2. Five lenses (use one before every HVO message or meeting)

| Lens | Ask yourself | Our protocol | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First principles** (Musk) | Strip our words away. What does this operator actually need? | P-041 Doctor Frame · the FPD in the Phase Action Protocol | Sofitel doesn't need "recovery drinks". It needs members to renew at RM2,999 a year. |
| **Working backwards** (Bezos) | What will the owner say to a friend 90 days from now? Write that first, then build the offer. | D-009 Clarity before Momentum | "Since RECUP came in, members stay after class and I know who's slipping." |
| **Two-way doors** (Bezos) | Is this ask easy to undo? Make the first yes small and reversible. | SYS-P058 · the tasting before the system | One tasting, one class, studio pays nothing. The RM9,000 setup is the one-way door, later. |
| **Leverage** (Naval) | Will this keep working when I'm not in the room? | P-042 Access > Hours · P-031 Phantom Equity | The check-in data from one tasting becomes the case study that sells the next ten studios. |
| **Asymmetry** (barbell) | Small, capped downside, large upside? | SYS-D025 Barbell | A tasting costs a morning and a few cups. The upside is RM9,000 plus RM1,200 a month. |

One more, always on: **specific knowledge** (Naval). 20 years on the floor is what nobody can copy.
Lead with it in person (D-001 Founder as Funnel), not with titles.

### 3. Structured borrowing: grow on other people's assets, with permission

| Borrow | From whom | How we pay it back | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distribution | The studio's members | A good experience and their numbers | Contact their members outside the agreement |
| Trust | The venue's brand (Sofitel, a premium studio) | We make them look good to their members | Use their logo or name in our marketing without written OK |
| Product and supply | Our nutrition partner | Volume, done by their rules | Name the brand publicly or make health claims |
| Proof | Members and owners | Credit, and a copy of the results | Quote anyone without permission. Never borrow a famous name we have no deal with |
| Tools | Outreach Console, Brain Pack, the deck, AI agents | Keep them updated (LEDGER) | Let a tool send for us without a person checking |

The rule behind all five: **borrow with permission, return more than you took, keep it in writing.**

### 4. Collaboration method (with any HVO)

1. **Before:** a one-page brief (T-029): who they are, what we think their problem is, the one ask.
2. **Agree the scorecard first:** what we will count on the day (checked in, cups, who wants more), and who sees it.
3. **During:** listen first (P-041). Write their words into the Outreach Console, not our summary.
4. **After, within one day:** send their numbers (V10), log the lesson, set the next step in the console.
5. **Every Friday (P-010):** one constraint, one change, one lesson.

### 5. The pre-flight (30 seconds, every time)

- Who exactly am I talking to: front desk, manager, owner? (Pick the matching reply gate.)
- What is their problem in their words, not ours?
- What is my one ask, and is it easy to say yes to?
- What will I record afterwards?
- Is anything I'm about to say untrue, unproven or not mine to use? Remove it.

### 7. What not to do with HVOs

- Don't send two offers in one message. Fuel first, system second (DECISIONS 20 Oct).

- Don't claim what you can't show: no borrowed fame, no invented numbers.
- Don't chase with volume. Chase with the ladder: gift, call, visit, three working days apart.
