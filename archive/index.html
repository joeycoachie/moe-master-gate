How the library works
    
    
      
  • Tabs are kinds of knowledge. Doctrines & Playbooks: the rules and the step-by-step. Tools: templates and prompts. Crucibles: real failures and what they taught us. Signals: outside ideas worth keeping.
    • 
      
  • Chips are the business area. SYS: the business. KIN: movement. MET: nutrition. Legacy: older items kept for history.
    • 
      
  • Search takes a question too, such as "what can't I say".
    • 
      
  • Use this template appears on any entry that has a ready-to-use version.

Add or correct an entry

    
Edit data/archive/protocols.json, tools.json or crucibles.json, then run npm run validate. Entries marked classified need links, questions, a status and a layer, or the check fails. Rules: data/schema.json and HANDOVER.md.
