RULE: no more than 2 sentences, then a question. Your only goal is a name and a time.
Say less than feels natural. Talking more does not build your frame. Asking does.

OPEN (warm, slow)
"Hi, this is Joey from [RECUP.STN]. I'm hoping you can help me."

THE ASK
"I'd like to speak to whoever [plans member events / looks after coaching]. Who's the best person for that?"

THEY ASK "WHAT'S IT ABOUT?" (one sentence, then stop talking)
"After one class, each member gets a recovery drink matched to their workout. We bring everything, there's no fee."

⭐ THE PIVOT (when they can't decide)
"Sounds like this isn't your call to make, and that's completely fair. Would it be a bad idea to tell me who does decide, so I stop bothering you?"
Why it works: "Sounds like…" names their situation, so they feel understood, not pushed. "Would it be a bad idea…" lets them answer with a no, which feels safe. "So I stop bothering you" makes helping you the easy choice.

CLOSE
"What's their name, and when are they usually in?"  Then: "Thank you, you've really helped."

AFTER (within 1 hour)
Note on the studio's Outreach card, in their words: who picked up · name you got · time you got · what they asked.
