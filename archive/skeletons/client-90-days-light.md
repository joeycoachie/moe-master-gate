CLIENT: [name] · START: [date] · 90-DAY GOAL: [one number] · PRICE: [RM]

NOT INCLUDED (say this in the proposal): weekly lesson sessions, custom playbooks.
They get: channel audit, content plan, lead-to-client steps, final report.

BRAND (trust)
[ ] Weeks 1-2: audit their channels: [link]
[ ] Weeks 1-2: fix the profile and first impression
[ ] Weeks 3-4: content plan with real proof (before/after, client stories with permission)
[ ] Weeks 5-12: weekly posts from material the owner gives us
[ ] Weeks 5-12: cross-promote from my accounts where it fits

COMMUNITY (local leads)
[ ] Partner list: nearby businesses for a shared voucher or event
[ ] One QR code or form that captures every local lead into the tracker

CONVERSION (no leaks)
[ ] Weeks 1-2: one tracker for all leads and sales
[ ] Weeks 3-4: lead-to-client steps written down
[ ] Weeks 5-12: pipeline worked toward the goal, tracker updated weekly

FRIDAY REPORT: numbers from all three (engagement, leads, conversion %) + what we did this week.
