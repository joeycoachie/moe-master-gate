1 · YOUR FLOOR (never go below)
- Direct cost per unit: [ingredients / your hour / materials] = RM__
- Fixed cost per run: [staff, transport, set-up, printing] = RM__
- Floor = direct cost × units + fixed cost + the profit you need = RM__

2 · THE OUTSIDE REFERENCE (what the buyer already pays elsewhere)
- 2-3 real prices with a source: [competitor / their own price list / Aura research]
- Where you sit and why: [below, level, above + one reason]

3 · THEIR VALUE (in their words, from the call)
- What it costs them now to NOT have this: RM__ / month (Mom Test Q2)
- So the price is small next to: [that number]

4 · THE SHAPE (pick one, Capital-Forward: money before work)
[ ] Fixed fee   [ ] Fee + per unit   [ ] Minimum + per unit   [ ] Partner rate (they bill, you invoice)   [ ] Retainer
- Who takes the risk if turnout or results are low? [them / us / shared]

5 · ONE NUMBER
- Price: RM__  ·  what's included: [ ]  ·  what's NOT included: [ ]
- Payment terms: [e.g. 50% to confirm, rest on the day]
- Founder approves with: decide: [offer] = RM__

CHECK: no invented numbers or guarantees · no partner brand · the floor is covered at the LOWEST turnout.
