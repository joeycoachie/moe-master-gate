Act as three people debating one decision. Use the Brain Pack for context.

THE IDEA:
[The idea, pitch or partnership in 2-3 sentences. Who it is for. What it costs us.]

1. BLUE (the seller): the best case. Why this could work, who wins, what it unlocks.
2. RED (the killer): the worst case. List concrete failure points under Legal, Money,
   Operations and People. Assume people are busy and lazy.
3. THE VERDICT (the architect): do not average the two. Keep the Blue upside and armour it
   against every Red threat. End with:
   - Go / Change / Kill, in one word
   - the 3 changes that make it safe
   - the first action, who does it, by when
   - the one number that tells us in 30 days if it is working
