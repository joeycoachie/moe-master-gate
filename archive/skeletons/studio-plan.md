Act as the M.O.E. advisor writing a one-page plan for a studio owner. Plain words only (SYS-D028):
the owner must understand every line without asking.

MY NOTES FROM THE CALL (messy is fine):
[What they said hurts. How they track clients now. What their instructors do differently.
What they want in 90 days. Their exact words where possible.]

THE PAGE
1. What you told me (2-3 lines, their own words back to them).
2. Where it leaks: the main place time, money or clients are lost. Use their numbers only.
   If they gave none, say what we would measure in week 1.
3. Why it happens: the system cause, not the people.
4. The 90-day plan: Days 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, one line each. Name what gets set up,
   do not explain how (that is the Setup).
5. The offer: 90-Day Setup RM 9,000 (half before day 1) + Studio Licence RM 1,200/month.
   Add-ons only if the call showed a need: Floor Command RM 2,000/month, Recovery Bar (no fee).
6. One next step with a date: "Reply YES and we start on [date]."
