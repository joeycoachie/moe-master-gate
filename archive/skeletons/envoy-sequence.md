WHO: [name, role, company]
THEIR BODY OF WORK (5 minutes on their site, Instagram, reviews):
- One thing they believe or say often: [ ]
- One thing they've built or are proud of: [ ]
- One problem they probably have that they haven't said out loud: [ ]

STEP 1 · FIRST MESSAGE (the hook: prove you see their real work)
"Hi [name], I saw [one specific thing from their body of work]. [One sentence on why it caught your eye.]
[One sentence on what you do, in plain words.]
[One small ask: a 15-minute call, or permission to send a short deck.]"

STEP 2 · FOLLOW-UP (3 working days later: add new value, never "just bumping this")
"Hi [name], following up with something useful instead of another ask. [A different piece of their work + one new idea, OR a gift: an invite to HQ, a free readiness check.]
[The same small ask, phrased as a no-question: Would it be a bad idea to…?]"

STEP 3 · BEFORE ANY PROPOSAL (take away their 3 biggest fears)
Their 3 most likely fears:
1. [e.g. "It will cost us and not work"]  → What answers it: [one class, two agreed numbers, stop if missed]
2. [e.g. "You're new, who are you?"]      → Proof they can check: [a client, a studio, a number with its source]
3. [e.g. "Hidden costs or catches"]       → Say exactly how the money works: [who pays, how much, when]
Say the fear out loud first: "The hard truth is, trying something new with your members takes trust."

STEP 4 · THE RESPECTFUL EXIT (end every sequence with it)
"If this isn't the right time, just say so and I won't follow up again. Either way, thank you for reading."

LOG IT: after each step, tap LOG on their card in the Outreach Console.
