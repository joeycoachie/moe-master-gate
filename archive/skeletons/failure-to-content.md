1 · EXTRACT: run the Crucible Investigator on what happened. Keep its public lesson.

2 · FORGE: the named fix (from the Investigator's Step 4) becomes the long piece.

3 · SHARE (plain words, SYS-D028, no names)
- Short post or Reel: the moment it went wrong, told honestly. End with a question.
- Long piece (newsletter): the fix, step by step, and how you would use it.
