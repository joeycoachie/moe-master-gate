RULE: they talk 70%, you talk 30%. Ask, then stay quiet.

0a · WHO WE ARE (same words every time): "RECUP.STN, a recovery station at the Health Bar in DC Mall."
0b · PICK THE FRAME (one offer per meeting):
- They already have their own app or website → fuel only, no system.
- Private / 1-on-1 studio, uneven traffic → an event, not a daily station.
- Busy group classes → the after-class station on their busiest class.
- Values-first gatekeeper → trust first: the Health Bar visit + the proof pack.
- Owner with a method and a gap → the system road (below).
0c · BEFORE YOU DIAL: write your role and offer in ONE breath (Single-Point Anchor). If you can't, don't call yet.
ROLE: "I'd be the [Pilates lead for your movement pillar]. Not staff, not a client: a partner who owns that piece."
OFFER: "[90-day block · 3-5 clients · I design, run and track · then we decide]."
(Crucible 18 Nov 2026: whoever names the role first owns the relationship. Leave it unnamed and the buyer names the cheapest role they already have.)

1 · FRAME (first 20 seconds, D-015: you set the agenda)
"Thanks for making time. Can I ask a few questions about how [business] runs, then share where I think I fit? If it's not a fit, we'll both know in 15 minutes."

2 · DIAGNOSE (SYS-P058 v2.1 Listen step, the Mom Test: ask about the past, never pitch portals or code)
- Q1 past action: "When did you last lose a client or a coach? What did you try to fix it, what did it cost you in time, and where did it fail?"
  (Nothing tried, nothing spent = a complaint, not a pain. Don't build for it.)
- Q2 cost: "Roughly what does that cost you each month, in lost clients or empty hours?" Get a ringgit number.
- Context: "Who looks after your clients between sessions: food, recovery, the days they're not with you?"

2b · IF THEY ASK "WHAT DO YOU OFFER?" (one breath, then stop. Write it before the call)
"[Their world in their words] covers [what they have]. I'd add [the missing layer]: [what you do], [how long], [for how many], then we decide."
Never list everything you can do. Vague = they define your role for you (Riad crucible, 18 Nov 2026).
If they say "follow me / help out for a while": time-box it and name the output. "Happy to. Two days, and at the end you get [a written plan]."

3 · LABEL (say back what you heard, in one line)
"Sounds like [everything runs through you / clients drift between sessions]."

4 · CLOSE (Q3 proof, then a yes or no on a paid pilot)
"If I ran your [Pilates block] for 90 days, what would you need to see before you put a fixed budget into it?"
Close with "What would you need to see to bring this to [the decision-maker]?" (not "what was most memorable?": crucible 20 Nov). Compliments are not data. "That's amazing, let's stay in touch" is a fail. If they offer you a discount, turn the talk back to their numbers.

DON'T: give two offers, talk prices before they name the cost, mention the setup fee, or close with "let's keep in touch".

AFTER (within 1 hour)
Note on their Outreach card, in their words: the cost they named · what they'd need to see · yes, no or a date.
