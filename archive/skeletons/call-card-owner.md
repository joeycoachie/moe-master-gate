RULE: they talk 70%, you talk 30%. Ask, then stay quiet.

1 · FRAME (first 20 seconds, D-015: you set the agenda)
"Thanks for making time. Can I ask a few questions about how [business] runs, then share where I think I fit? If it's not a fit, we'll both know in 15 minutes."

2 · DIAGNOSE (P-041: no pitching yet)
- "When did you last lose a client or a coach? What happened?"
- "What did you try to fix it? What did it cost you?"
- "Roughly what does that cost you each month?"
- "Who looks after your clients between sessions: food, recovery, the days they're not with you?"

3 · LABEL (say back what you heard, in one line)
"Sounds like [everything runs through you / clients drift between sessions]."

4 · CLOSE (a yes or no on a paid pilot)
"If I ran your [Pilates block] for 90 days, what would you need to see to pay for it?"

DON'T: give two offers, talk prices before they name the cost, mention the setup fee, or close with "let's keep in touch".

AFTER (within 1 hour)
Note on their Outreach card, in their words: the cost they named · what they'd need to see · yes, no or a date.
