## The five steps

1. **Find:** 10 venues that care about results but run on WhatsApp and spreadsheets.
2. **Say hello:** one short message, one ask (a 15-minute call or the deck).
3. **Listen:** ask, say back what you heard, no pitch. Script below.
4. **Plan:** a one-page 90-day plan from their own words (T-029).
5. **Start:** sign the 90-Day Setup, or book the RECUP.STN pilot.

---

## Step 3 · Listen (the Mom Test)

Ready to read before a call: **Call card 2 · Owner or decision-maker** (Archive → Use a template).

**Rules**
- Never pitch portals, dashboards or code in this step.
- Ask about the past, not the future. People are kind about ideas, so compliments are not data.
- High rapport with zero revenue is an illusion (Ries). Count what you learned and what was paid.

**Q1 · Past action**
"When did you last try to fix this? What did you actually do, what did it cost you in time, and where did it fail?"
No time or money spent means a complaint, not a pain. Do not build for it.

**Q2 · Cost**
"What does this leak cost you each month, in lost clients or unbooked hours?"
Get a ringgit number.

**Q3 · Proof**
"What would you need to see before you put a fixed budget into a pilot?"

**Close**
End on a yes or no to a paid pilot (the 90-Day Setup). "That's amazing, let's stay in touch" is a fail.
If they offer a discount or treat you as their customer, turn the talk back to their numbers.

**Bad vs good questions**

| Bad (future, opinion) | Good (past, fact) |
|---|---|
| "Would a booking and diagnostic portal be useful?" | "How do you handle it now when a high-value client drops off?" |
| "Would you pay monthly for software that saves time?" | "When did you last lose a client or a trainer to scheduling friction? What did it cost?" |

**After (within 1 hour):** on their Outreach card, in their words: the cost they named · what they'd need to see · yes, no or a date.
